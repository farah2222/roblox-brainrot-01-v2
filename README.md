# Brainrot Rumble

A 3D brainrot game for make.fun's [“Make the best AI Roblox brainrot game” bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/roblox-brainrot-01) ($1,000, top 50 paid). Built by an AI agent in a recorded session.

**The game:** In a neon night-market arena, absurd meme-born characters march down a glowing conveyor belt. Buy one with cash, **carry it home in your hands** — run to your base before the tsunami hits or a thief bonks you — and it earns money every second. Climb the ladder, fight back with weapons, steal from rivals, defend your base, survive the tsunami and the meteors, spin the wheel, finish the mission chain — and rebirth for more.

Every brainrot shows its name, rarity and $/s on a floating label, and each one has its own funny quote in the Index. Mutations — **Golden (x2)**, **Lava (x4)**, **Rainbow (x5)**, **Diamond (x10)**, **Glitch (x12)** — can roll on any spawn, and a Secret spawn can arrive as a **boss**: twice as big, wearing a crown. A rarity ladder with all 7 tiers and all 5 mutations is always on screen, top-left.

**The Brainrot Index [I]:** press I (or the INDEX button) to open the full collection — all 40 brainrots with name, tier, $/s and their quote. Ones you haven't discovered yet show as a "???" silhouette. Finish a whole tier for a **+$3,000** collection bonus; discover all 40 for **+$50,000**.

**The mission chain:** 10 missions in a row — first buy, earn $1,000, earn $10,000, lock your base, spin the wheel, survive a tsunami, rebirth once, own 5 brainrots at once, bonk the thief, find a Secret. Each one pays cash on completion, and finishing all 10 pays a **chain bonus of $25,000 plus a permanent +25% income**. The current mission is always on screen ("MISSION n/10").

**The loop:**
- Brainrots spawn at the west gate and march east; walk up and press **E** to buy one (costs cash) — then you **carry it in your hands** (you walk 20% slower while carrying) and run it to a free pen on your base. Delivering it pays a **delivery bonus** by tier (up to $5,000 for a Divine). Drop it, get bonked, or get hit by a weapon and you lose it.
- A delivered brainrot earns its $/s, ticking every second — your HUD shows cash and $/s
- Press **V** near one of your brainrots to **sell it** for 70% of its cost (times its mutation)
- Buy the next tier, then the next; shop upgrades, each with its own level shown in the shop: **Speed Boots** (+30% walk speed, LV 1–5), **Money Magnet** (+12% $/s, LV 1–5), **Golden Glove** (−20% weapon cooldowns, LV 1–5), **Base Expansion** (+1 pen per level, LV 1–2, up to 10 pens)
- **Rebirth** (from $30K, cost x4 each time): reset cash and brainrots for a permanent +30% $/s each, with confetti

**The fight — weapons and stealing:**
- **Slap Glove [1]** — smack a rival flying backwards (3s cooldown)
- **Freeze Ray [2]** — freeze a rival solid in an ice block for 3 seconds (9s cooldown)
- **Brainrot Magnet [3]** — yank the nearest belt brainrot straight to your feet (12s cooldown)
- **Steal [F]** — stand next to a rival's pen and hold the channel for 5 seconds to take their brainrot; a weapon hit, wandering off, or a base lock breaks it. A stolen brainrot is carried home the same way — and if you get bonked while carrying it, it goes back to its owner
- **Lock your base [L]** ($600, 75 seconds): a red energy gate seals your plot against thieves and stealers
- **Bonk the thief:** hit Si Cilok Nakal with any weapon while he is sneaking and he drops what he is carrying and runs off — it counts toward the mission chain

**The threats:**
- **Si Cilok Nakal**, the thief: every ~80 seconds he sneaks in from the edge, walks to a random unlocked base and needs 7 seconds to steal its richest brainrot — touch him to shoo him away, or **bonk him with a weapon** while he is carrying it and he drops it
- **The Giant Noodle Tsunami**: every ~160 seconds a 15-second warning counts down, then a towering noodle wall with a foamy crest sweeps the belt (unbought brainrots wash away) and shoves every player backwards — dash home with your catch. Survive one and it counts toward the mission chain
- **Meteor noodles**: every ~200 seconds flaming noodles rain down and leave glowing pickups worth cash where they land
- **Golden Noodle Hour**: every 4 minutes the market turns gold for 60 seconds — **income x2** and every spawn rolls its mutation **4 times** and keeps the best. The first one starts 45 seconds after you join, so the gold rains early

**The lucky spin wheel [Q]:** one free spin every 2 minutes — cash, a free brainrot, a mutation upgrade on a brainrot you own, or 2x income for 60 seconds.

**The world:** a procedurally built 3D night-market arena — central belt with rollers and neon rails, four fenced home plots with pen markers and lock gates, a spawn plaza, a shop kiosk, a giant noodle-bowl statue with chopsticks, sambal trees, drifting clouds and a spin-wheel platform with a golden wheel. A real 3D character spawns and walks it (`CharacterAutoLoads=true`).

**The HUD:** cash and $/s top-centre (clear of Roblox's top-left menu buttons), rebirth count, base fill and multiplier, wave countdown, thief alerts, a weapon bar, and a shop panel (lock / rebirth / upgrades / spin). The **rarity ladder** (7 tiers + 5 mutations) is always on screen top-left with an INDEX button; the **current mission** and a **carrying banner** sit under the cash bar, and a gold banner announces Golden Noodle Hour. Effects: buy particles, floating cash popups, screen shake on the wave and on hits, confetti on rebirth, freeze ice blocks, steal progress bar.

**Structure (one module per job, clean client-server split, state in one place):**
- `src/shared/BrainrotCatalog.luau` — the cast, rarities, mutations, spawn rolls
- `src/shared/RumbleConfig.luau` — every tuning number
- `src/shared/GameState.luau` — the single place that holds a player's state
- `src/shared/MissionCatalog.luau` — the 10-mission chain and its rewards
- `src/shared/MoneyFormat.luau` — money and clock formatting, server and client
- `src/server/WorldBuilder.luau` — builds the 3D arena at runtime
- `src/server/ConveyorService.luau` — spawn, march, buy, place on base
- `src/server/EconomyService.luau` — the money tick, lock, upgrades, rebirth
- `src/server/CombatService.luau` — weapons, steal channels, knockback, freeze
- `src/server/ThreatService.luau` — Si Cilok Nakal, the tsunami, the meteor shower
- `src/server/SpinService.luau` — the lucky spin wheel
- `src/server/init.server.luau` — builds the world, creates the RemoteEvents, starts the services
- `src/client/Game.client.luau` — keys, effects, popups, camera shake, confetti
- `src/client/Hud.client.luau` — the whole ScreenGui and its live updates

**Build it:** `rojo build default.project.json --output game.rbxl` (Rojo 7.7.1), then `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts` to hand it in. Luau verified with the Luau CLI 0.747 (`luau-compile` passes on all 14 scripts).

**Play it:** open `game.rbxl` in Roblox Studio and press Play. Publish from Studio to get the play link in `PLAY.json` (the owner publishes it — see below).

**Status:** the game is published on Roblox as 'Brainrot Rumble' — play it at https://www.roblox.com/games/71817809365134/Brainrot-Rumble (place 71817809365134, universe 10777604225), with the play code `makefun-orbit-cloud-lumber` in its description.
